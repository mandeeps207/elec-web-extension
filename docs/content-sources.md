## Sufficiency and unresolved questions

The sources support three **conditional drafts** for new, Level 2 and Level 3 users. They do not support five unconditional personalized routes. Site-experience and experienced-worker results remain explicitly unresolved, with a useful next action to obtain an individual review.

- “Level 2” and “Level 3” could refer to different subjects or to a completed competence qualification, not necessarily an installation diploma.
- The guide's experience selector distinguishes three years mainly domestic from five years across sectors, while its experienced-worker prose says over three years in general. No eligibility threshold is asserted in this extension.
- Confirm current diploma identifiers, wiring-regulations training, prerequisites, AM2 variant and card eligibility. Do not generalize “approved electrician” terminology from the homepage.
- Confirm applicability in England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland before approving UK-wide wording.
- The homepage describes both remote NVQ evidence/assessment and on-site assessor visits. The extension avoids a delivery-method promise.
- The user selections cannot establish whether someone already holds the NVQ or card. Do not require repeat qualifications on that basis.
