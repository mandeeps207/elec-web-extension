# Privacy disclosure draft

Applies to the local 0.1.0 implementation. Pending company approval and confirmation of an official public policy URL. Do not claim this file is a published policy.

The qualification checker processes your starting-point selection locally while its popup is open. It does not save that selection. Closing the popup discards it.

The extension does not collect personal information or browsing data, transmit selections, use analytics or advertising, set cookies, or make background network requests. It does not read web pages or browsing history. It has no account, form, database, storage access or browser permissions. Scripts, styles, qualification data and icons are local to the extension.

These statements are supported by the runtime and manifest audit described in `docs/verification.md`. They concern the extension, not browser vendors, extension stores or the linked website.

When you choose an external link, a normal new tab visits `https://elec.training/` or its electrician guide. A website visit involves normal browser network traffic and the website's own privacy/cookie practices. The extension adds no selection data, tracking parameters or referrer to those links.
