## Extension name

UK Electrician Qualification Checker – Elec Training

## Short summary

See a typical next step in your electrical training with Elec Training.

## Long description

Use the Elec Training checker in Microsoft Edge to explore where your current training or experience may lead. Pick one of five starting points and read a short, ordered result without leaving the extension popup.

Results explain the limits of the available information as well as possible next steps. Some starting points need a review of your existing certificates and site experience before a route can be identified. The checker also links to the Elec Training homepage and detailed qualification guide.

No login is needed and selections are not saved. This is an informational tool, not a certificate assessment or a guarantee of qualification or card eligibility.
