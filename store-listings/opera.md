## Extension name

UK Electrician Qualification Checker – Elec Training

## Short summary

Explore your electrical training starting point in a compact toolbar popup.

## Long description

The Elec Training qualification checker gives Opera users a quick way to explore typical electrical training progression. Select the option that matches your current position to see a concise explanation, possible steps and important limitations.

You can compare the five starting points by returning to the selection screen. When experience or a qualification level alone is insufficient, the result directs you toward an individual review. Two links provide access to the official Elec Training website and its full guide.

Guidance runs locally in the extension. No account or saved profile is needed. Individual routes vary, and the checker does not assess your certificates or award qualifications.
