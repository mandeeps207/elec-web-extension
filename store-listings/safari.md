## Description

Explore typical UK electrician qualification routes from your Safari toolbar with Elec Training.

Choose the starting point that best describes you: completely new, completed Level 2, completed Level 3, gaining site experience, or an experienced electrician. Read a concise route overview, ordered next steps and important caveats. Where your starting point alone cannot identify an appropriate route, the checker explains what needs individual review.

- Five clear career starting points.
- Local qualification guidance with relevant limitations.
- Simple reset to explore another starting point.
- Links to Elec Training's website and full qualification guide.
- No login, purchase, saved selections or analytics.

The checker provides general guidance. It does not verify certificates or guarantee qualifications, employment, course admission or ECS card eligibility, and it does not replace individual professional advice. Qualification guidance runs locally; opening the linked website requires an internet connection and the website's own privacy practices apply.
