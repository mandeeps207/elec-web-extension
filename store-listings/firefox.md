## Long description

The UK Electrician Qualification Checker from Elec Training puts a concise training-route overview in your Firefox toolbar. Choose the starting point that best describes you, then read the suggested progression and its limitations in the popup.

The checker covers new starters, Level 2 and Level 3 diploma holders, people gaining site experience and experienced electricians. Where a single answer cannot establish the route, the result explains what needs an individual review. The example diploma route introduces workplace evidence, NVQ, AM2 and checking ECS Gold Card eligibility.

You can return to the choices at any time or open Elec Training’s website and full guide in new tabs. The checker works locally, without an account or saved selections. It offers general guidance; it does not verify certificates or determine that you are qualified.
