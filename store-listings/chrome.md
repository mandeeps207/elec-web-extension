## Extension name

UK Electrician Qualification Checker – Elec Training

## Short summary

Understand typical electrical training steps with a simple local checker.

## Long description

Open the Elec Training checker from your Chrome toolbar and select your current position in your electrical career. Five starting points cover beginners through experienced electricians.

Read useful guidance inside the popup, including ordered steps where a typical route can be described and clear limitations where personal circumstances need further review. Choose a different starting point whenever you wish. Links to Elec Training and the full electrician qualification guide open in new tabs.

The extension requires no account, requests no browser permissions and does not save your selection. Its purpose is to explain possible training progression, not to certify competence or confirm eligibility.
